# Peugeot 308 Allure: four powertrains

This is a historical French new-car comparison, using the **2 May 2024**
catalogue. All four were offered together as 308 hatchbacks in Allure trim,
with automatic transmissions. The electric version is sold as E-308. Its
power and some standard equipment differ. The hybrid is **48 V, non-plug-in**.
These are not current 2026 offers or used-car valuations.

## Adjustable assumptions

Cash purchase in month 1, **60 months**, **15,000 km/year**, identical mileage.
The upper bound of each engine's WLTP range is a consistent starting point,
not measured road consumption or a claim about every Allure configuration.
No additional charging-loss multiplier is added to WLTP electricity consumption.
Energy prices stay fixed; they are not a forecast of five future years.

Monthly energy provision is `annual km / 12 / 100 × consumption × unit price`,
rounded to cents: **€131.28 petrol, €107.74 diesel, €47.49 electric,
€111.25 hybrid**. Electricity assumes **100% charging at home**. Change the
energy row for your mileage, driving conditions or public-charging mix.

| Estimated budget, not a quote | Petrol | Diesel | Electric | Hybrid |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Insurance/year, shared row | €840 | €840 | €840 | €840 |
| Maintenance and tyres/year | €500 | €550 | €350 | €500 |
| Charger and installation, once | — | — | €1,500 | — |

Annual provisions are converted to monthly amounts rounded to cents. Insurance
is linked across scenarios; engine-specific expenses remain independent.

The car is recorded once as an asset at its purchase price in month 1. A common
illustrative **−15% nominal annual depreciation**, divided by twelve, reduces
that asset monthly from month 2. This is a sensitivity assumption, **not a
predicted resale price** or an annual effective rate. There are 59 depreciation
steps over this horizon. No sale income is added: that would count the residual
value twice.

## Reading and limits

This isolates car costs without income or initial savings. Negative cash
therefore represents spending, not a household affordability assessment.
With the default nominal display, **economic cost = expenses − residual asset
value = −net worth**. The normal cash warnings still apply. Global inflation
and display preferences remain active; the source table always shows the
original baseline, even after edits. Source amounts are EUR; changing a
currency label does not convert them.

Before discounts, subsidies, registration tax, CO₂/weight purchase taxes,
financing, the existing household electricity subscription and any power
upgrade. Add these for a personal purchase decision. No engine is asserted to
be the cheapest for every driver. Existing projects are not rewritten; the
example can be added from the project template shelf in either interface mode.
